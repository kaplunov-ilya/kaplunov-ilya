## Hi, I'm Ilya 👋

**Unity Client Developer** · C# · 4 years in commercial gamedev: mobile and cross-platform midcore games, online CCG.

Currently working on the client of **[Berserk Heroes Online](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4926310/Berserk_Heroes_Online/)**, a cross-platform online CCG for PC, iOS and Android.

### What I do

- 🧱 **Client architecture:** modular structure, DI, logic/view separation, systems that grow without rewrites
- 💰 **Meta & monetization:** shops, gacha with pity, battle pass, personal offers, regional payment routing
- 📱 **Cross-platform UI:** separate PC/Mobile layouts, aspect ratios from 4:3 tablets to 21:9 and foldables
- ⚡ **Mobile performance:** profiling, GC spikes, batching and draw calls, stable on 2 GB RAM devices
- 🤖 **AI-assisted workflow:** Claude Code & Codex for tests, pre-MR review, codegen and comparing solutions
